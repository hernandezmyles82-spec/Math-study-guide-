# Unit 2: The American Revolution

## Key terms
- **Patriots**: colonists who supported independence from Britain
- Loyalists - colonists who stayed loyal to the British king
- Boston Tea Party: a 1773 protest against the tax on tea

## Important events
- The Declaration of Independence was adopted on July 4, 1776.
- Thomas Jefferson was the main author of the Declaration.
- The war ended with the Treaty of Paris in 1783.
- The colonists won the Battle of Saratoga, a turning point of the war.
- France became an ally after the American victory at Saratoga.

Q: Who was the commander of the Continental Army?
A: George Washington
